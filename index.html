Evidence base: general display screen equipment (DSE) and ergonomic guidance comparable to established occupational health frameworks such as HSE DSE guidance · general electrical safety principles for extension leads and home equipment · WHO guidance on physical activity and sedentary behaviour
This checklist is educational general home office and work-from-home safety guidance to help you build strong, confident habits around ergonomics, electrical safety and lone-working. It is not formal occupational health and safety training, a workplace risk assessment, or a substitute for your employer's policies or local regulations, which vary by employer and location. If you have concerns about your specific home-working setup, please consult your employer's health and safety policy, an occupational health advisor, or a qualified professional for a formal home-working risk assessment.
